Frequently asked questions

Short, honest answers.

Does CTRMAX require an account?

No. CTRMAX never asks for a login, email, or password. You open the app and start journaling.

Where is my data stored?

Exclusively on your iPhone, in iOS’s encrypted local storage. Nothing is sent to a server, and nothing leaves the device unless you choose to export it yourself.

Does CTRMAX connect to my bank or brokerage?

No. CTRMAX does not execute trades, fetch live market data, or integrate with any financial institution. It is a journaling tool — you enter the numbers; CTRMAX keeps them safe.

Is the app a financial adviser?

No. CTRMAX is a private journal for your financial life. Nothing in the app constitutes investment, tax, or legal advice. A clear in-app disclaimer reminds you of this every session.

How do I back up my data?

Use the in-app CSV export to create a local backup you control. You can also back up your device through iTunes or iCloud as you normally would.
