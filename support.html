Frequently asked questions

I lost my data after reinstalling CTRMAX. Can you restore it?

CTRMAX does not store your data on any server, so we have nothing to restore from. If you have an iTunes or iCloud device backup taken while CTRMAX was installed, restoring that backup to your device may bring your journal back. We also recommend using the in-app CSV export regularly as a personal backup.

How do I export my data?

Open CTRMAX → Settings → Export. Choose CSV, pick a destination (for example, the Files app, Mail, or another share-sheet target), and confirm. The file is generated locally and handed to the share sheet — nothing is sent anywhere automatically.

Can I sync CTRMAX across multiple iPhones or iPads?

CTRMAX is currently a single-device journal. Each device holds its own data. If you switch devices, use the in-app export to move your journal.

Does CTRMAX connect to my bank or brokerage?

No. CTRMAX is a journaling tool. It does not connect to financial institutions, does not fetch live market prices, and does not execute trades.

How do I delete my data?

Delete CTRMAX from your device. All journal data stored by the App is removed with it. If you previously exported a CSV, remember to delete that file from wherever you saved it.

Does CTRMAX show investment advice?

No. CTRMAX is a private journal for the numbers you record. Nothing in the App constitutes investment, tax, legal, or financial advice.

I have a feature idea. Where do I send it?

We love hearing from users. Send feature ideas to karlawsokunevajob68@gmail.com with “Feature idea” in the subject line.

Response time

We are a small team and we read every message. Most support emails receive a personal reply within a few business days. If your issue is preventing you from using the App, please mark your message “Urgent” in the subject line.
