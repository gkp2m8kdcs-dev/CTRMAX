Privacy Policy

Your data stays with you. Period.

Last updated: January 2026

Short version: CTRMAX does not collect personal data, does not require an account, does not use third-party analytics, and does not sync anything to a server. Everything you record lives on your iPhone, encrypted at rest by iOS.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how CTRMAX (“the App”, “we”, “our”, “us”) handles information in connection with your use of the App. We have designed CTRMAX from the ground up around a single principle: your financial journal belongs to you, not to us.

2. Information we do not collect

  • No account information. CTRMAX never asks for your name, email, phone number, or password.
  • No financial credentials. CTRMAX never asks for bank logins, brokerage credentials, or card numbers.
  • No transaction data on our servers. All transactions, receipts, holdings, budgets, and notes stay on your device.
  • No usage analytics. CTRMAX does not contain third-party analytics SDKs, ad networks, or trackers.
  • No device fingerprinting. CTRMAX does not collect advertising identifiers or device fingerprints.

3. Information stored on your device

CTRMAX stores the following information locally on your iPhone only, in iOS-protected storage encrypted at rest by the operating system:

  • Transactions, categories, and notes you create.
  • Receipt photographs and document scans you attach.
  • Investment holdings, cost basis, current price, and free-form notes.
  • Budget targets and progress data.
  • App preferences such as currency, theme, and category customisation.

This information is never transmitted to us or to any third party. If you delete CTRMAX, this information is removed with it, subject to the standard behaviour of iOS backups on your device.

4. Permissions CTRMAX may request

CTRMAX only requests the operating-system permissions it needs to perform actions you explicitly initiate. None of these permissions are used to collect data about you:

  • Camera — so you can photograph receipts and statement documents directly into your vault. CTRMAX does not record video and does not upload images.
  • Photo Library (read) — so you can attach existing receipt images and document scans to your transactions. CTRMAX does not browse or scan your library beyond the image you select.
  • Photo Library (write) — so you can save exported reports and receipts to your photo library when you choose to export them. CTRMAX never writes anything to your library without your action.

5. Backups and exports

CTRMAX offers an in-app CSV export that writes a file to a location you choose (for example, the Files app or a share-sheet destination). This export is created and controlled by you; CTRMAX does not transmit the file anywhere automatically.

CTRMAX does not include its own cloud backup. Any device-level backups you make through iTunes, Finder, or iCloud are governed by Apple’s privacy practices and your own settings.

6. Children’s privacy

CTRMAX is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because CTRMAX collects nothing at all, this is a structural guarantee, not a procedural one.

7. International use

CTRMAX operates entirely on your device. Because no personal data is transmitted to us, data-protection regulations about cross-border transfers simply do not apply to your journal contents.

8. Changes to this policy

If we ever change this Privacy Policy in a material way, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, surface a notice inside a future release of CTRMAX. Because CTRMAX does not know who you are, we cannot email you about changes.

9. No financial advice

CTRMAX is a private journaling tool. Nothing in the App constitutes investment, tax, legal, or financial advice. You are solely responsible for the accuracy of the information you record and for the financial decisions you make.
